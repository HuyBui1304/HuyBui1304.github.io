Case study 2 · Power BI · 2026

Olist E-Commerce Analytics Dashboard

A seven-page report on 99,092 orders from Brazil's Olist marketplace (Jan 2017 – Aug 2018, R$15.4M delivered sales). Each page answers one question: how sales are trending, whether delivery is fast and on time, what drives bad reviews, how concentrated sellers are, which categories grow, whether customers come back and how they pay.

Power BIDAXPython ETLStar schemaPBIP / TMDL
View the project on GitHub ↗

Key findings

  • 1.7★average review for orders delivered more than a week late, against 4.3★ for on-time orders. Late delivery (6.8% of orders) is the clearest driver of bad reviews.
  • 67%of sales come from the top 10% of sellers; the 19 sellers above R$100K alone bring in 20%.

  • Built the report as a Power BI Project (TMDL + PBIR) with a custom dark theme, sidebar navigation and slicers synced across pages.

Case study 5 · Power BI · Python · 2026

Vexere Train Ticket Funnel Analysis

A take-home analysis of Vexere's train ticket product, built from 398,121 raw web tracking events (1 Mar – 24 Apr 2026): 193,768 searches from 113,847 devices and 4,887 orders. The three-page dashboard follows the flow from search to payment, and the report for the Product Owner and Marketing team shows where searches come back empty and where orders stall. Shown as delivered, in Vietnamese.

Power BIDAXPython (pandas)Event dataPBIP / TMDL

Key findings

  • 27.7%of searches return no trips. The largest group that can be identified, 19.4% of them, carries a travel date that has already passed.
  • 17.6%of trip selections go on to a seat on that same trip, and fewer do when only a few seats are left or the fare is high.
  • 15.0%of orders with a back action on the payment page are recorded as paid, against 63.8% of orders without one.

My work

  • Cleaned the raw events in pandas: removed duplicate events, standardised place names, and rebuilt searches, 30-minute sessions and orders, then reconciled row counts and keys against the raw file.
  • Built the three-page dashboard as a Power BI Project (TMDL + PBIR) with DAX measures and a custom dark theme.
  • Linked each trip selection to a seat selection on the same trip and session rather than comparing step counts that do not nest, and wrote a report with the problem, likely cause, fix and metric to track for each finding.
