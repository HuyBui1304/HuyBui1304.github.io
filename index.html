Case study 1 · Power BI · 2026

Retail Sales Analytics Dashboard

A full-year report on 1,650 retail order lines (₫1.11B revenue). Each page answers one management question: where margin is lost, which customers are slipping away, and which marketing spend pays off.

Power BIDAXStar schemaPower Query
View the project on GitHub ↗

Key findings

  • −14.3 ptMargin falls from 53.0% at no discount to 38.7% at 25% off, while units per order stay flat. Deep discounts cost margin without adding volume.
  • 28.2%of revenue sits with 140 customers inactive for 90+ days; the 39 high spenders among them form a ready win-back list.
  • 2.2×higher cost per order on social commerce than on the website.

My work

  • Cleaned the raw workbook in Power Query and modelled it as a star schema.
  • Wrote 132 DAX measures (margin, weighted discount, repeat rate, at-risk logic, cost per order), with definitions shown on each page.
  • Designed a landing page and six analysis pages with consistent filters.
Case study 2 · Power BI · STS, 2026

Vietnam Textile & Garment Trade Intelligence

Power BI reports on Vietnamese customs declarations for textiles and garments. Sourcing and sales teams use them to size markets and find suppliers and buyers by market, company and HS code.

HS 50–6352 Cotton54 Filament55 Staple fibre60 Knitted fabric61–62 Apparel
Power BIPostgreSQLETLCustoms data

Key findings

  • $38.3bnexported vs $20.2bn imported in 2023; apparel (HS 61–62) alone is $21.4bn of exports.
  • $6.2bnof knitted fabric imported, the largest import line and twice what is exported: garment makers still rely on foreign fabric.
  • 58.9%of 2022 exports to China were cotton, so China mainly buys inputs from Vietnam.

My work

  • Built an ETL pipeline: web crawl → Google Drive staging → PostgreSQL.
  • Standardised company names, HS codes, incoterms and payment terms so totals reconcile across views.
  • Designed lenses by market, company, product and HS code, and wrote company briefs from the same data for the sales team.
